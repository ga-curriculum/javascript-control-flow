### Nested branching statements

Sometimes, you'll encounter situations where one condition depends on another. In such cases, you can use nested `if` statements. These are `if` statements placed inside other `if` or `else` blocks, allowing for more complex decision-making.

Clarity and simplicity are essential when using branching in your code. Complex nested conditions can be challenging to understand, so strive for readability.
